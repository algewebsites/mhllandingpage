My story

Hi, I’m Alissa. I know sleepless nights.

“Better sleep didn’t start in my bedroom. It started with how I spend my evenings, and my life.”

For years, I had trouble falling asleep. On chaotic evenings, I’d lie awake for ages. On other nights, I’d fall asleep completely exhausted, only to wake up a few hours later and not be able to fall back asleep.

What changed it wasn’t one magic trick. I built a routine that lets my nervous system come down: places, people and things that genuinely feel good to me. Yoga, sound healing, time with myself. And tiny habits I believe everyone can incorporate.

That’s exactly how I work with my clients: we start with your nights, and let the rest of your health follow.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the Better Sleep Call really free?

Yes. 30 minutes, free and without obligation. It’s for both of us to see whether working together makes sense. Either way, you’ll leave with a first step for tonight.

Who is this coaching for?

For women 30+ whose sleep suffers from overthinking, stress, a full life with kids and work, or changing hormones, for example in perimenopause. If you lie awake, wake up tired or can’t switch off, you’re in the right place.

Do I need a wearable or sleep tracker?

No. If you already track your sleep, we can use that data. If not, how you feel in the morning is all we need.

Is this medical advice or therapy?

No. I’m a coach, not a doctor or therapist. If you snore loudly or stop breathing at night, have slept badly at least 3 nights a week for 3 months, or feel low most days, please talk to a doctor first. Coaching can complement treatment, but never replace it.

What happens after the call?

If we’re a good fit, I’ll send you a proposal for a coaching program tailored to you, including duration, rhythm and price. You decide in your own time.

Is the coaching online?

Yes, all sessions take place via video call, so you can join from anywhere. Coaching is available in English and German.
