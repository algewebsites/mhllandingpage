The 1-page sleep reset

Why you wake up tired Even after 8 hours.

Hours in bed aren’t the same as recovery. Use this page to find what’s draining your nights, then fix it with one small experiment over 7 nights. With or without a wearable.

30-second check

Is your sleep recovering you?

  • You wake up tired after 7+ hours in bed
  • At night you feel wired, your mind won’t switch off
  • You often wake up between 2 and 4 a.m.
  • You need caffeine to feel normal
  • If you track: your resting heart rate runs higher, or your HRV lower, than usual

3 or more ticks? Your sleep isn’t recovering you yet. Let’s find out why.

4 hidden reasons you wake up tired

Most people have one main one. Which is yours?

The Wired Mind

Your body is tired, but your nervous system is still on duty.

Feels like
Racing thoughts, to-do lists in bed, 3 a.m. wake-ups
In your data
Higher overnight resting heart rate, lower HRV

Try: the 10-minute switch-off ritual

The Late-Night Fuel

Alcohol, late caffeine or a late dinner keep your body working at night.

Feels like
You fall asleep fine, but wake up unrefreshed
In your data
Resting heart rate up and HRV down on those nights

Try: 7 clean evenings

The Shifting Clock

Your sleep times keep moving, so your body never knows when night starts.

Feels like
Mini jet lag every Monday, wide awake on Sunday night
In your data
Bed and wake-up times that vary by an hour or more

Try: one fixed wake-up time

A hidden sleep issue

Sleep apnea, chronic insomnia or hormonal changes, for example in perimenopause.

Feels like
You’re tired no matter what you change
In your data
Often nothing clear, and that’s the point

Try: talk to your doctor (see below)

Your 7-night experiment

Pick one. Change nothing else. Then track.
  1. 1Switch-off ritualLights low, phone out of the bedroom, to-do list on paper, then 10 minutes of slow breathing (in 4, out 6).
  2. 27 clean eveningsNo alcohol, last caffeine before 12 p.m., dinner 2 to 3 hours before bed.
  3. 3One fixed wake-up timeSame time all 7 days (±30 min), daylight within an hour of waking.
  4. 4Feel-first checkAlready doing all of the above? Just track how you feel each morning.

Feel first, check second. Rate how you feel before you look at any score. When your feeling and your score disagree, trust the feeling, and judge the experiment by the trend over 7 nights, never by one night.

Bonus: did it work?

Ask your AI after night 7.

Here are my 7 nights: [paste your tracker or a screenshot of your app’s weekly view, without your name]. My experiment was [your experiment]. Compare nights 1–3 with nights 5–7. Did my morning energy, sleep or resting heart rate change? Explain it simply, and don’t diagnose anything.

Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or your wearable’s own AI assistant.

Please don’t skip this

Talk to a doctor if…

  • you snore loudly, gasp, or someone has noticed breathing pauses
  • you’ve slept badly at least 3 nights a week for 3 months or more
  • you’re exhausted no matter what you change
  • your sleep changed with perimenopause and it’s affecting your life
