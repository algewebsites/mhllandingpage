Your 1-page sleep reset

The Late-Night Fuel You’re asleep. Your body is still working.

Alcohol, late caffeine or a late dinner keep your system busy at night. You might fall asleep easily, but your heart works harder and your sleep gets lighter, especially in the second half of the night. The good news: this type often changes fastest.

Sound like you?

Tick what you recognise

  • You fall asleep fine, but wake up unrefreshed
  • A glass of wine is how you switch off
  • Your last coffee, tea or matcha is after lunch
  • Dinner often happens late, close to bedtime
  • If you track: resting heart rate up, HRV down after drinks or late dinners

2 or more ticks? Then the tips below are made for you.

What’s going on
Your body is still processing alcohol, caffeine or a big meal while it should be recovering.
Feels like
Sleeping, but not resting. Waking up in the early hours. Groggy mornings.
The good news
Small changes to your evenings can show up in how you feel within days.

Your tips to sleep better

Start tonight. Pick one or two, not all five at once.
1
Alcohol-free eveningsTry one week without alcohol. Keep the ritual: a nice glass, but with something alcohol-free.
2
Coffee cut-off at noonLast coffee, black tea, matcha or energy drink before 12 p.m.
3
Dinner 2 to 3 hours before bedIf you get hungry later, have something small and light.
4
Drink during the dayGet most of your water in before the evening, so you don’t wake up thirsty or for the bathroom.
5
A new evening ritualHerbal tea, a short walk or a book instead of the late snack or drink.

What to notice

How rested you feel when you wake up, and whether early-morning wake-ups change.

Feel first, check second: rate how you feel before you look at any app.

Please don’t skip this

Talk to a doctor if…

  • you snore loudly, gasp, or someone has noticed breathing pauses
  • you’ve slept badly at least 3 nights a week for 3 months or more
  • you’re exhausted no matter what you change, or feel low most days
  • your sleep changed with perimenopause and it’s affecting your life
