2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., San Francisco, USA. When you visit the website, Netlify’s servers automatically process technical data so the page can be delivered and kept secure: your IP address, date and time of the visit, the page requested, browser and operating system, and the referring website. These logs are stored by Netlify for a short period and are not combined with other data.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in a secure and working website). I have a data processing agreement with Netlify. More: netlify.com/privacy

The fonts on this website are stored on my own server. No connection to Google Fonts or other font services is made.

3. The sleep test

The sleep test runs completely in your browser. Your answers and your result are not sent to me or anyone else and are not saved. When you close the page, they are gone.

4. Sleep reset and emails (Brevo)

If you sign up for your personal sleep reset, you can download it right away. I process your email address and your sleep type (for example “The Wired Mind”), so that my emails can fit your result. You will also receive my My Healthy Longevity emails with sleep and health tips and occasional information about my coaching.

  • Double opt-in: after signing up, you get an email asking you to confirm. Only after you confirm are you added to my list. I save the time of sign-up and confirmation as proof of your consent.
  • Provider: I use Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, Paris, France). Your data is stored on servers in the EU. I have a data processing agreement with Brevo. More: brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy
  • Email statistics: Brevo records whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, so I can understand which content is helpful.
  • Unsubscribe: you can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the end of every email, or by emailing me. Your data is then deleted, except for a minimal record needed to prove your earlier consent.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

5. Better Sleep Call request

If you request a free Better Sleep Call, I process the details you enter in the form: your name, email address, what is affecting your sleep most, your sleep type (if you took the test), your preferred days and time slots, and anything else you choose to share.

Information about your sleep can count as health data. I only process it because you explicitly agree to this in the form, and only to prepare and hold your call.

  • Form: the form is processed by Netlify Forms (Netlify, Inc., USA), and I receive a copy by email.
  • Video call: the call itself takes place on Zoom (Zoom Communications, Inc., San Jose, USA). Zoom processes your name, email, IP address and technical meeting data. I do not record calls. More: zoom.us/privacy
  • Storage: I delete your request no later than 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a client. Client data is kept as long as needed for the coaching and as required by tax and accounting law.

Legal basis: steps prior to entering into a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and your explicit consent for health information, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw your consent at any time by email.

6. Contact by email

If you email me, I process your email address and your message to reply to you. My emails are stored with an external email provider that processes them on my behalf. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. I delete emails once they are no longer needed, unless there are legal retention obligations.

7. Transfers outside the EU

Netlify and Zoom may process data in the USA. These transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified, and on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

You have the right to:

  • get information about the data I store about you (Art. 15 GDPR)

  • restrict the processing of your data (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • receive your data in a common format (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Just email me at alissa@myhealthy-longevity.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. The authority responsible for me is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), aki.ee. You can also contact the authority in your own country.

9. Changes

I update this privacy policy when my website or services change. The current version is always available on this page.
